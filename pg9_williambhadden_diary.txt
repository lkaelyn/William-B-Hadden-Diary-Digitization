13th The day being very calm I with many more lay down to 
repose the body to have a chat about some of our previous knowledge 
and after talking we fell into a sort of slumber, I saw through my 
sleep a lovely female coming riding upon the top of the white waves, in 
front of a dreadful gale which was rapidly approaching our gay bark. She 
had a smile upon her lips & playing with her locks, who was in yellow as the
Marygowulls & hung no importance over her speech but our ship’s helm to leeward she came close to said it and cried out to the steersman, upon the sorrow who was making all preparations for the storm, if you 
could deacend show but the man who saw & heard her to his fancy 
through his sleep, she called to me why do you sleep young man do you not 
fear Neptune, nor his anger, I replied, Mr. Neptune never gave me any 
reason to fear him & I have weathered his wrath many years. 
I admire your faith she said & with a smile said do you admire his 
daughter Miss Neptrona. I do was my reply, but alas you sir has cost me many a bitter pang of disappointment, she 
replied, your sorrows shall be few & your dangers

17th. Again we are in almost a calm, how unfortunate, we have not had 
6 days fair sailing since we left Kurrache, & now we are on board 44 days,
what will the end be, it ought to be good according to the old saying, today a ship 
appears but in an other direction than the one we saw yesterday, apart many 
small whales & porpoises was seen rolling about in the calm waters near our ship,
about 5 oc P.M. we came up with the ship, it was very pleasant for both, she 
was from Java for Liverpool. Her name is the Rinbrow Shire. She was then 17 days at sea. 
Come give us them cheers we returned the compliment, we are very crowded in such
I must say this only helps for an evening amusement is the for castle, our 
authorities ordered us to be put off there at retreat where could we go, we 
could not move about, in the dark & all being experienced with their commands to 
kick up a row out & shouting & calling him there over board,they feared 
mutinee & gave orders if you would not stop after a note or two more was to shoot. A

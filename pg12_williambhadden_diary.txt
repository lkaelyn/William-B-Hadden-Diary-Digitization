18th got better pretty fair today but during the night the wind 
fell off & we was again in almost a calm. My prayer is for 
a brisk wind to lead us on over the waters to the land that we are so 
anxious to meet, now at the land of our Nativity. 
14th very slow sailing today at 12 noon we were 170 miles 
from the Line. The sun is exceedingly hot & prickly heat very

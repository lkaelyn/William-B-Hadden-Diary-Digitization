13th. Today the sun came from behind the waters very clear & as 
he came up the wind freshened & we hoisted stunsails & we went away at 
a rate of 7 or 8 miles per hour, the day appears very clear, the air is getting 
cold now we are obliged to lay past the Indian clothing now, & take in
the European kind, we will have a apart of 3 winters this year, as we 
had Dec Jany & March the Indian winter, May of the Cape winter & 
we will have the commencement of winter at home, the day is very short 
now in this part of the world the sun does not rise before 7 oc
The winter is quite at the opposite time of the year from what it is at home.

14th. very good sailing last night today the wind is a little started but 
still we are sailing about 7 & 8 miles per hour, today I am on guard &
have a nice view of the unagitated skies in the evening & morning 
when all is still, I am never wearied admiring the works of lofty nature.

15th. Today I am again aloft although we have no accidents, yet we have 
orders to attend to the same as if we were in the field in short 
in the honorable system of our Army. No wonder the men are thy ways.

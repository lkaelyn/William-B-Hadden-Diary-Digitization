Family of Kelsons
Father, married twice, first wife had four children—two sons, two daughters.
Eldest, Laurence, joined the Navy, second, Henry, same early.
Eldest daughter, Mary Ann, married Mr. Symons, a wealthy gentleman.
Youngest, Louisa, married Mr. G. H. Garst.
Second wife’s children—youngest son, George James.
Daughters, Maria and Sarah.
Mrs. Kelson’s residence: North Street, Manchester.
Master, 9 yrs. old, of delicate constitution.
Visited by Miss Amelia Hendricks.
Died after brief illness.
Buried in the cemetery near the churchyard gate.
Great grief to the family.
Friends expressed deep sympathy.
Servants all much attached.
Father, Mr. Kelson, never recovered spirits after his son’s death.
This is the last entry.

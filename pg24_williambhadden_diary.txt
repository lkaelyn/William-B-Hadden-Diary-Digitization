raw. Poor man he had undergone 2 Campaigns, & endured the hard
ships of the climate & dangers, & this far on his way home to see 
his people and had to part with his existence about 1000 miles from 
the Cape of Good Hope. In God who ordains these things & we only
may say that we hope God has taken from a world of many troubles
to one who's skies are celestial & what about the savants of God.

8th very slight winds today, scarcely sailing 60 Miles in 24 hours,
but before night I hope a breeze will come & send us over the waters a little farther,
about 1 OC PM. one of our Stansal Rooms was broken by to hard packing.

9th about one o.c. A.M. the wind brisked up & sent our ship along 
at a rate of about 7 or 8 knots per hour, when we rose in the morning how 
bright were every man’s countenance as their eyes beheld the long smile
not orders & the Ship speeding along so rapidly, the sailors are 
busy today preparing new Sails & forward it’s more.

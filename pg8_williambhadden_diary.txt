Very good times pass cheerily on board of ship. The Captain is a right good fellow and always steady & friendly. Mr. Williams & Lt. Cole same way, also Mr. Jones and Mr. Fraser. Only wrote to my brother & sister. My relation for to find. Signed this day for a kind remembrance.
All together we spent most delightfully their time in singing & dancing when the H. Chesters tunes the flute. Lt. Murray, Collier, Major Collier, Jones, Lt. Battelle, Jones, Lieut. Hadden, Mr. W. Fraser, Mr. L. Battelle, Mr. H. Hughes, Miss L., Miss W., Miss L., and many others.
Indian with songs & songs and music. Our English soldiers join very readily & joyfully. Signed for memory sake.
W. B. Hadden.

4th We sailed 179 miles but we have got out of the Tropics for some time. 
Today at 10 1/2 we had Church Service as usual, yesterday we had a lot of rain 
& strong sails put up, I believe that is the Custom before coming into the Cape
Seas as they are generally very rough, but I do not think that we will have 
worse than we have had; I cannot keep from laughing at the Purdie opinions 
of some of our passengers, yet they are to be pitied, they know no better, as 
their education has been neglected. Some would come up & say as suspected
as they could good morning sergeant, when will we get home do you think, 
will we reach England in a fortnight do you think, I replied we will 
not do that my good fellow, we have about 12,000 miles yet, & may be 
be as glad to labour & storm, with a sigh he would say well I am quite
kicked about hear, resigned, & my very stuff’d for nothing, dinner that 
is all, and the worst is there is a force coming coming going, and like to 
make two hearts into one about my delay, & God knows I cannot 
go & when for the life of me. Would you know the favour

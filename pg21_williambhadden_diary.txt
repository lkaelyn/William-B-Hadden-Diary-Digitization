5th We are still sailing at our usual speed. Seas very calm, 
plenty of sails set & full of wind every man singing blow 
ye winds, your pleasant breeze, & fill our white boomy sails. we went 
over the distance of 250 miles this 24 hours.

6th Bad luck to the man on the Bulean today for the wind has almost 
died away & we are rolling about upon a calm Sea. but there is a black 
Cloud on our Larboard side & we expect a breeze from it. we are now 
sailing S. W. by W. & this is our proper direction untill we round the Cape— 
every ones countenance is very distressed like to dry in consequence of the wind 
leaving us, their anxious gaze over the bulwarks would mark the stiffless of the 
winds have compassion on them. I again feed their wishes with full sails.

whilst gazing over the bulwarks, there appeared as shore of small blackish 
helping out from amidst the waters, & shortly after a very large Whale approach
coming after them & every few seconds appearing half above the waters, she 
must have been about half as large as the ship. several large shark was 
also seen sailing beside our ship & showing at times her belly

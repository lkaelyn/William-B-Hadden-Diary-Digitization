solid, exceedingly dear, especially tobaco, Today the weather is inclined to be 
squally, & showery, but we care but little as long as the wind continues. 
Towards night a ship appeared in our front & before dark we was opposite
although a good bit distant, she was signalled & answered with the enemy’s flag. 
She apparently was sailing towards the Mauritius, the wind stabilized towards 
night, but by 10 o.c. P.M. the breeze commanded to gather & by 10 it was 
sending us along at the rate of 5 or 9 miles per hour.  

10th Still a strong wind but almost ahead of us. Heavy shower of wind

Passage from India to 
England

On the 3rd of April 1815 we embark at Kirrachee 
East India for England on board the Ship Yorkshire 323 in 
number with 5 or 6 Cabin passengers, the ship is now a 
Bark but on the account of so many soldiers leaving
India shipping was very scarce so the government had to 
put as many on each ship as could possibly be taken 
so we had one hundred to many on board. I was very 
much crowded, our Berth was of good. yet after a few 
days at sea we could have put with little more. 
We was greatly amused looking at the flying fishes as 
they rose from before our ship & flew until their wings 
got dry & then they lighted on the waters again. The 
distance they generally fly is about 30 or 40 yards. 
We also was amused very much with the 
Dolphins as they played away on the surf.

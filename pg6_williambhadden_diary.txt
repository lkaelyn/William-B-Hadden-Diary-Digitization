The water pressing the smallest fishes, generally the flying 
tribe, many of our men never saw any of either species 
& had some very comical remarks regarding them. There 
was also many whales of all species to be seen making the 
water fly up above them like water spout, there was to 
be seen near where they were lots of them grazing and 
floating upon the sea in great herds.
And the first two days we sailed very fast & passed 
several small boats passing from Bombay to Kirrachee, nothing 
of any consequence until the 7th of April when a man 
Angus Kinnald of the 19th jumped over board and was 
never seen again, he was for sometime previous insane & 
rather was over him he passed the  he was over three or four times deposed the sentry was 
upon the ship at about 1/2 past 12 AM. immediate orders was given & about lowered. he was 
never seen any more & the Sentry being much 
neglected of duty, poor old Burwin a sad termination of his service 
for upwards of 21 years.

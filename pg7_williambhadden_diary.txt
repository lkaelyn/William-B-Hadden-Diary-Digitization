Today is the 10th day on board of ship for us & we are now 
lying becalmed below the line under a fearful hot sun. 
I have horribly to compare with the British Isles. 
11th Today we have a brisk and gliding along at the rate of 
from 9 knots per hour we have passed the line last night and
very happy as we are now getting into a colder climate. 
we are well off any way with the exceptions of being 
too much crowded. our rations are very good and are as follows, 
Tea or coffee for breakfast, biscuit, 1/2 lb. of salt beef with 4 oz. of 
flour made into duff, one day for dinner with a small bit 
of mustard & vinegar, next day for dinner we have 4 oz. of 
salt pork with a pint of pease soup, next day for dinner we have 4 oz. 
of preserved beef with 8 oz. of rice. Tea or coffee for supper 
every night we also after dinner get a pint of beer or a
spoon of rum, and a gram of lime juice. 
We have some very good sport trying to harpoon 
the passengers chiefly, play around our ship & we are very
much amused with the flying fish & the dolphins.

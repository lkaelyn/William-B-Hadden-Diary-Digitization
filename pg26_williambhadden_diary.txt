deck where they were met by waves of water coming washing

12th It is not so stormy today but wind very unsteady some times 
the sails are flapping about the mask right about a woman's legs with
out sometimes we are sailing about 6 miles per hour, 
the wind has keeped very little all day but shifted a little more to our 
favour & the sea is quite smooth now & we glide along
